Category 01

Land Clients & Close Deals

1
The Irresistible Cold Pitch
Sales
Turns a blank page into a personalized pitch that gets replies.
You are an expert B2B copywriter. Write a short, friendly cold outreach message to [TYPE OF BUSINESS]. I offer [YOUR SERVICE]. Mention one specific problem they likely have, offer one small free value upfront, and end with a low-pressure question. Keep it under 90 words, no jargon.
2
Proposal in 60 Seconds
Sales
Generates a clean, professional project proposal instantly.
Act as a freelance consultant. Create a project proposal for [CLIENT/PROJECT]. Include: overview, scope of work, timeline, deliverables, pricing options (3 tiers), and next steps. Tone: confident but approachable.
3
Objection Crusher
Sales
Prepares you for the "it's too expensive" moment.
A potential client said: "[THEIR OBJECTION]". Give me 3 calm, non-pushy responses that reframe the value and move the conversation forward. Include one question I can ask back.
Pro Tip

Always add "ask me 3 questions before answering" to any prompt when you want the AI to tailor the output to your exact situation.

Category 02

Content That Goes Viral

4
30 Days of Content Ideas
Content
Never stare at a blank content calendar again.
You are a viral social media strategist. Give me 30 content ideas for [NICHE] on [PLATFORM]. Mix formats: hooks, tips, myths, stories, and "how-to". For each, write a scroll-stopping first line.
5
The Hook Machine
Content
The first line decides if people keep watching/reading.
Write 10 different scroll-stopping hooks for a post about [TOPIC]. Use curiosity, bold claims, and relatable pain points. Keep each under 12 words.
6
Long-Form to Short-Form
Content
Turn one piece of content into a week of posts.
Here is my blog post / video script: [PASTE TEXT]. Break it into 7 short social posts, each with a hook, 2-3 value lines, and a call to action. Keep my voice.

Category 03

Email & Outreach

7
The Welcome Sequence
Email
Build trust with new subscribers on autopilot.
Write a 3-email welcome sequence for new subscribers to [YOUR BRAND/NEWSLETTER]. Email 1: warm welcome + quick win. Email 2: my story + why I do this. Email 3: soft pitch for [OFFER]. Friendly, no fluff.
8
Re-Engage Cold Leads
Email
Wake up subscribers who stopped opening.
Write a short "we miss you" email to re-engage subscribers who haven't opened in 60 days. Include a curiosity subject line and one clear reason to come back. Under 120 words.
9
Subject Line A/B Tester
Email
Better subject lines = more opens = more sales.
Write 10 subject lines for an email about [TOPIC]. Mix curiosity, urgency, benefit, and personal styles. Mark which 2 you'd test first and why.
Stack Them

Chain prompts: generate the email, then say "now write 5 subject lines for this" and "shorten it for mobile". The AI keeps context.

Category 04

Marketing & Ads

10
The AIDA Ad Writer
Ads
Proven copywriting formula, done for you.
Write ad copy for [PRODUCT] using the AIDA formula (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action). Target audience: [AUDIENCE]. Give me 3 versions of different lengths.
11
Landing Page Copy
Ads
A full page of conversion copy in one go.
Write landing page copy for [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Include: headline, subheadline, 3 benefit bullets, social proof section, FAQ (5 Qs), and a strong CTA. Persuasive, clear, benefit-focused.
12
Customer Avatar Builder
Ads
Know exactly who you're selling to.
Create a detailed customer avatar for [PRODUCT]. Include demographics, goals, fears, daily frustrations, where they hang out online, and the exact words they'd use to describe their problem.

Category 05

Productivity & Automation

13
Inbox to Action Plan
Productivity
Turn a messy email into clear next steps.
Here's an email I received: [PASTE EMAIL]. Summarize it in 2 lines, list any action items with deadlines, and draft a professional reply.
14
Meeting Notes Magic
Productivity
Raw notes become a tidy, shareable summary.
Turn these rough meeting notes into a clean summary with sections: Decisions Made, Action Items (with owners), and Open Questions. Notes: [PASTE NOTES]
15
Weekly Planner
Productivity
Get organized in seconds, not hours.
I have these goals this week: [LIST GOALS]. Build me a realistic day-by-day plan, batching similar tasks, with buffer time and one "most important task" per day.
Privacy Reminder

Don't paste sensitive client data, passwords, or private personal info into public AI tools. Use placeholders or anonymize first.

Category 08 · Bonus

Prompt Power-Ups

Add these phrases to ANY prompt to instantly get better results. These are the multipliers the pros use.

Get tailored output

"Ask me clarifying questions before you answer."

Improve quality

"Give me your first draft, then critique it and write a better version."

Match your voice

"Here are 2 samples of my writing — copy this tone: [paste]."

More options

"Give me 5 wildly different angles, not variations of the same one."

Stay concise

"Be concise. No intro, no summary, just the answer."

Think first

"Think step by step before giving the final answer."

The Meta-Prompt

Stuck? Ask the AI to build the prompt for you: "Act as a prompt engineer. Write me the perfect prompt to [your goal]." Then run that prompt.
