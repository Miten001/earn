Who can take part

  • UK residents only, aged 18 or over at the time of entry.
  • A valid UK email address and a UK delivery or billing postcode where requested.
  • One entry per household. Duplicate or automated entries are removed.
  • The questionnaire completed in full, with accurate answers.
  • Employees of the promoter, its partners and their immediate families are not eligible.

How selection works

  • Qualifying entries are added to the £100 shopping voucher draw.
  • Selection is made at random. The number of entries received affects the likelihood of selection, and taking part does not guarantee it.
  • Selected entrants are contacted by email using the address supplied at entry.
  • We will never ask you for payment, a password or remote access to your device.
  • Full programme terms, including the response deadline, are shown before you submit.

Straight answers

Questions UK shoppers ask first

The details that matter, in plain English — before you decide whether to take part.

Does taking part cost anything?

No purchase is necessary to submit an entry, and the questionnaire never asks for payment card details. Any optional third-party offer presented after the questionnaire is shown with its own terms before you agree to anything.

Is this an Amazon promotion?

No. This is an independently operated UK consumer rewards programme. Amazon is a third-party retailer: Amazon and the Amazon logo are trademarks of Amazon.com, Inc. or its affiliates. This website is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Amazon.

How likely is it that I'm selected?

Selection is random from qualifying entries, so it depends on how many entries are received in the draw period. Completing the questionnaire does not guarantee selection, and no outcome is promised to any entrant.

How will I be contacted?

By email, using the address you provide at entry. Add the sender domain to your safe list and check your spam folder. We will never ask you to send money, share a password or install remote-access software.

What happens to my answers?

Your questionnaire answers are used to understand UK household shopping preferences and to administer the draw. Where you separately opt in, relevant offers may be shared by our partners. You can withdraw consent at any time using the unsubscribe link in any email or by contacting us — see our Privacy Notice for the full detail.

What if I don't live in the UK?

This programme is open to UK residents aged 18 or over only. Entries from outside the UK cannot be accepted and are removed during validation.
