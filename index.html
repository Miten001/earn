Frequently Asked Questions

Is this GST invoice generator really free?

Yes. 100% free, no signup, no watermark, no limits. Your data stays in your browser.

Is this invoice GST-compliant in India?

Yes. It includes GSTIN, HSN/SAC code, CGST/SGST/IGST split, invoice number, date, and place of supply — all mandatory fields under GST law.

When should I use IGST vs CGST + SGST?

Use IGST when seller and buyer are in different states (interstate supply). Use CGST + SGST when both are in the same state (intrastate). Toggle the checkbox in the form to switch.

Is my data safe?

Yes. Everything runs in your browser. There is no server. Your invoices and customer data are never uploaded anywhere.

Can I use this on mobile?

Yes. The tool is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets, and desktops.

What is HSN/SAC code?

HSN (Harmonised System of Nomenclature) is for goods, SAC (Service Accounting Code) is for services. Mandatory on GST invoices above certain turnover thresholds.
