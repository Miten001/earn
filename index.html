Low-effort and passive income ideas — honest effort & risk labels.

Note: No income is truly "without work". Every idea below requires some setup time, money, or skill. Beware of anyone promising free money.

Avoid These Scams

  • "Get rich quick" courses or coaching
  • Paid surveys promising hundreds per day
  • Crypto / forex signals groups asking for fees
  • MLM / pyramid recruiting schemes
  • Anyone asking you to pay to start working
