## Step-by-step on Gumroad

1. Go to **gumroad.com** and create a free account.
2. Click **New product** -> choose **Digital product**.
3. **Name** -> paste the Product Title above.
4. **Price** -> set $7 (or toggle "Pay what you want").
5. **Upload** -> add `Make-Money-Online-Ebook.pdf`.
6. **Cover/Thumbnail** -> upload `cover.jpg`.
7. **Description** -> paste the description block above.
8. **Tags** -> add the tags above.
9. Click **Publish** and share your product link.

## How to drive traffic (the "high traffic" part)

- **Free lead magnet:** Make a short 3-5 page "lite" version and give it away to build an email list, then sell the full guide.
- **Short-form video (Reels/Shorts/TikTok):** Post 1 tip per day from the book. Link the product in bio.
- **Reddit / Quora / Facebook groups:** Genuinely answer "how do I start making money online" questions, mention the guide where allowed.
- **Pinterest:** Pin the cover image with keyword-rich titles - great for evergreen traffic to digital products.
- **Bundle / upsell:** Offer 1-on-1 coaching at a higher price to buyers who want personal help.
